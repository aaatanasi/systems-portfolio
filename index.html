Dashboard

One place to track appointments, analyze sales, and review CRM activity. Google Calendar feeds the dashboard through Google Sheets, and the workflow syncs records to the CRM.

How the integration works

Google Calendar → Google Sheets → Dashboard → CRM

Apps Script syncs appointment data and connects the reporting and CRM workflows. A potential next step is letting users edit CRM records directly from the dashboard. The public version uses sample data and does not connect to live accounts.

Customer Email Automation

I built a Google Sheets and Apps Script workflow to send individual customer emails and track send activity in one place, without relying on Gmail’s built-in mail merge.

From a custom menu in Google Sheets, the team could select a recipient group, update the message template, check the remaining sending quota, and send batches of pending messages. Per-recipient status and a 24-hour check against Sent helped prevent repeat sends.
